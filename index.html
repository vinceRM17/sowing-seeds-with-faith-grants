📖 What you're looking at

This is a grant pipeline dashboard for showing funding opportunities specifically tailored to its mission. It's not a generic Google scrape — every entry was researched, scored, and prioritized for 's actual context.

What's in it & how these opportunities were selected

Selection methodology — every entry passes these filters before being added:

Selection criterionWhat it means for this organization
Mission fitDirect alignment with the organization's programs and target population. Funders with documented history of granting to peer orgs are prioritized.
Tier sizingOpportunities are right-sized to the organization's scale and capacity, with a few larger stretch/relationship funders flagged as monitor-only.
Geographic eligibilityKY-only funders, regional, and national funders that fund KY orgs are all included. Mismatches are flagged or pre-archived.
Capacity matchReporting burden, match requirements, and federal compliance friction are scored. Heavy stretches are pre-archived (toggle "Show archived" in Scoring Matrix to surface them).
Multi-source verificationEvery priority funder was verified against multiple sources. The Research tab shows the organization's actual grants-received history — proven funders are flagged.

⭐ What the stars mean

Stars are a priority flag placed by hand during the research pass. They are not calculated from the score.

⚠️ The stars are an emphasis marker, not the ranking. They were placed by hand and several unstarred opportunities score close behind the starred ones. To rank, sort by Weighted Score in the Scoring Matrix. To see real giving history, use the Research tab.

How to use this — 4-step workflow

  1. Glance at the MOST URGENT card at the top of the Scoring Matrix or Calendar — that's the deadline you can't afford to miss this week.
  2. Open the Scoring Matrix next. Opportunities are ranked by weighted score. Tier 1 (4.0+) are the realistic 2026 portfolio. Tier 2 (3.0-3.99) are stretch + relationship-building. Click column headers to re-sort.
  3. Check the Calendar tab for the 60/90/180-day windows and what to work on this week vs. plan for later.
  4. Reference the Research tab before approaching any funder — proven recurring relationships (top of the table) are highest-priority renewals.

⚠️ What this is NOT

  • Not a list of guaranteed wins. Every grant is competitive. Scores are estimates of fit and likelihood, not promises.
  • Not a definitive ranking. Adjust the weight sliders in the Scoring Matrix to reflect the organization's priorities at any given moment — the rankings will reorder live.
  • Not a substitute for the organization's own funder relationships. Personal connections, peer references, and existing donor stewardship beat any algorithmic match. Treat this as a research starting point, not a substitute for relationship work.
  • Not a static document. Funder priorities, deadlines, and applicant eligibility change. The dashboard is refreshed quarterly + ad-hoc when major funder events trigger updates.

    Time–Saving Features (All in the Scoring Matrix Tab)

    FeatureHow to use
    Click any score to editScores are editable 0–5 number inputs. Click and type a new value — the weighted total recalculates instantly.
    Adjust scoring weightsUse the sliders near the top to change how much each criterion contributes to the total. The "Reset to Standard" button restores the default (25/15/20/20/15/5).
    Category filter barClick any category pill to hide/show that category. Use the "All" / "None" buttons to quickly toggle everything.
    Archive a grantClick the × button on any row to archive it (decided not to pursue, missed deadline, etc.). Toggle "Show archived" above the table to bring them back. Archived grants don't affect Tier 1/2 counts.
    Edit a deadlineClick any deadline in the Deadline column and type the new date. A dashed underline and "edited" tag indicate your edits. Custom deadlines persist across reloads.
    Resize columnsDrag the right edge of any column header to resize it. Widths are remembered. Click "Reset Columns" to restore defaults.
    Sort any columnClick a column header to sort by that column. Click again to flip direction.
    Open any grant linkClick the opportunity name to open the funder's application page in a new tab. Hover to see grant size and quick description.
    Inline notes per grantAdd a one–line note inline next to a grant in the scoring matrix or calendar. Same data also appears in the "My Notes" tab under "Grant–Specific Notes."
    Export notesOn the My Notes tab, click "Export Notes" to download everything as a timestamped .txt file. Useful for sharing with board members or archiving.

    CadenceWhat to do
    WeeklyCheck the Calendar tab's "Act Now" section for deadlines in the next 60 days. Review inline notes on in–progress applications.
    MonthlyRe–score Tier 1 and Tier 2 grants to reflect shifting priorities. Archive anything that's been declined or missed. Update the Research tab's action items.

    Troubleshooting & FAQ

    IssueFix
    My edits disappearedScores/notes/deadlines save to the specific browser you're using. Switching browsers or devices won't carry them over. Use Export Notes + re–import manually, or use the same browser consistently.
    The table looks crampedDrag column edges to resize. Click "Reset Columns" if it gets messy.
    A deadline says "Past"Click it and edit to the new cycle's date. The grant won't be archived; it'll just update.
    I want to add a new grant not in the listThis dashboard is hand–curated at build time. Request an update with the new grant and scoring context.
    I want to share with a colleagueShare the URL. They'll see the underlying grants but start with blank scores/notes. Use Export Notes + email the .txt to share your annotations.
    I'm on mobileThe dashboard is fully responsive — tabs, tables, and forms adapt to phone screens. Editing works on mobile; a larger screen is just roomier for heavy scoring sessions.
